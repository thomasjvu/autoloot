# Quick walkthrough

## 1. Connect your channel

Open **Settings → Twitch**. Enter your app credentials, copy the displayed callback URL into the Twitch developer console and authorize your channel.

## 2. Publish seller details

In Settings, add your seller name, support/privacy email, shipping regions, returns policy and retention notice. Review what buyers see before accepting real orders.

## 3. Add a lot

Choose **Inventory → Browse catalog**, or **Add a lot** to enter an item manually. Review identity, set/number, actual condition, quantity and opening price. Catalog scans are reference images, not proof of your copy's condition.

## 4. Set up checkout

Use your own Stripe account. Follow the payment setup FAQ and integration guide, then complete a sandbox checkout and refund. Switch to live credentials only after verifying the payment webhook. Set shipping costs and allowed destinations.

## 5. Prepare your stream

Choose an OBS layout in Settings and copy its Browser Source URL. Use one source for cohost voice. Open the storefront in a separate browser to check the buyer flow.

## 6. Run a lot

Start an auction in Studio. Registered viewers bid with `!bid 25.00` in Twitch chat. Standard auctions extend late bidding; sudden-death auctions keep a fixed deadline. The winner receives a private order. Confirm payment before shipping, then add tracking.

## Optional: in-app tour

Choose **Settings → Take a tour** for the interactive walkthrough. You can skip it or replay it later. This written guide is available without starting the tour.

## Optional: practice first

Use **Settings → Practice show → Load sample lots** for simulated bids and payments. No real funds move. Clear practice content before your real show.
