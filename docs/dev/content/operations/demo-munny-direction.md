# Munny-hosted submission demo

Creative direction for the under-three-minute demo. Supersedes the human narration and opening in [the initial recording script](/docs/operations/demo-recording). Target 2:45 at 1920×1080, 30 fps. Use OBS and actual Twitch footage for the demonstration.

Munny hosts the whole film with original character babble and readable dialogue text. No spoken narrator. Use her existing character artwork and rig. The illustrated shop is a framing scene; the demonstration shows the actual application.

## Dialogue and character treatment

- Warm cream dialogue panel, dark high-contrast text, soft irregular outline and a small name tab. Use Autoloot’s typography and colors with an original design inspired by the pacing of Animal Crossing dialogue.
- Reveal at about 28 characters per second, with short punctuation pauses. Hold completed pages for at least 1.5 seconds; extend holds for longer lines. Keep most pages below 90 characters and two lines.
- Original short pitched syllables, varied in small deterministic patterns. Babble only while letters arrive; pause at punctuation and stop on completed pages. Mix below meaningful app sounds. Do not use extracted game audio.
- Small speaking bounce, mouth changes and occasional blink. Hold still between lines. Use a welcoming gesture in the intro and a restrained celebration at the auction win.
- During demonstration shots, use a compact Munny portrait and dialogue panel in available screen space. Keep prices, timer, chat commands, approval actions and payment status unobstructed. Move the panel between cuts when necessary.
- Let the complete dialogue remain visible while the demonstrated action happens. Avoid typing across the critical bid or payment transition. No second subtitle track repeating the panel.
- Do not imply the edited narration is a live app response. Actual event-driven Munny reactions can remain in the footage, with overlapping babble muted in the edit.
