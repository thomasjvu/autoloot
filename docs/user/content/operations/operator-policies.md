# Operator policies and AI disclosures

The bundled Terms, Privacy and Help pages describe the current software. The person/business running an installation supplies its public seller/operator identity, support/privacy email, shipping/fulfillment policy, returns/refunds/dispute policy and retention notice through Settings → Seller identity & policies. Those values persist in show data and appear in the storefront and legal pages. Use a suitable public business contact. Missing details are explicitly flagged; the software does not invent a legal entity or contact.

Publishing requires an explicit operator acknowledgment; the server records its timestamp and notice version. Only administrator-authorized requests can change these fields. Publishing is blocked during an armed/paused run or active auction so the operator must resolve/stop that activity before changing policies. New auction and buy-now orders snapshot the operator policy and notice version. Buyers can view that snapshot under their order. Existing orders are not retroactively assigned policies. Publishing a page, recording an operator acknowledgment or taking a snapshot does not establish buyer consent or legal enforceability.

## Responsibilities and limits

Terms distinguish the independent operator/seller from the software provider while recognizing they can be the same entity in a deployment. Sellers are responsible for their listings, rights to goods/data/content, format eligibility, connected accounts, pricing, taxes, permissions, automation scope, physical fulfillment, customer support, refunds and disputes to the extent applicable law assigns those duties. Software/data/AI availability is not a guarantee of authenticity, value, legality, profits or fulfillment. Mandatory consumer/privacy rights and non-excludable provider obligations remain intact.

AI limitations are displayed in Companion, cohost cards, automation authorization and the public storefront. Overlay labeling identifies a software or AI cohost. Buyer sign-in links to Terms and Privacy alongside seller-policy acknowledgment text. These are disclosures; this release does not introduce a comprehensive versioned buyer clickwrap audit or universally valid consent process.

## Privacy accuracy

The Privacy page describes local files, uploads, order and run records, session cookies, widget local storage, public bid usernames, private order access, payment references, AI prompt/image transmission and provider dependencies. Retention notices describe the operator's actual practices; the software has no automatic personal-data deletion schedule. Reservation expiry, sign-out, Twitch disconnect and demo cleanup are not full deletion. Operators must include exports/backups and any crash-left temporary files when applying retention or responding to requests.

No claim of universal controller/processor status, lawful basis, zero provider retention, no model training, or absolute security is made. Additional hosting, logging, providers, jurisdictions, age restrictions and data rights need deployment-specific review. Operators must supply required notices and implement the practices they promise.

## Before real sales

1. Publish accurate operator details and transaction/retention policies.
2. Review the bundled language against the actual deployment, applicable laws and provider terms; obtain appropriate legal review before relying on liability or consent language.
3. Verify the public storefront/legal pages show the correct operator and the captured order policies remain stable after changes.
4. Implement required eligibility, consent, retention/deletion, incident-response and request-handling procedures. A notice alone does not perform these operations.

This release makes disclosures available and records operator policy settings. It does not certify legal compliance or make all responsibility transferable to end users.

Reference guidance: [FTC privacy/security](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security) emphasizes honoring privacy promises; [FTC advertising disclosures](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business) explains that buried or easily missed qualifications may be ineffective. These references inform placement/accuracy, not a jurisdiction-specific legal opinion.

## Editable presets

Seller settings offer TCG singles/slabs, sealed TCG and general merchandise starting points. Fill draft preserves nonempty policy fields unless Replace existing policy text is checked, never changes identity/contact, and clears acknowledgment. All text remains editable. Replace bracketed durations/destinations/retention details before publishing; the server rejects unresolved placeholders. No preset auto-publishes, certifies compliance or performs deletion/refunds.
