# Inventory

## Create a lot

In Inventory, add a title, description, opening price, actual condition, and stock quantity. Upload your own product photo when available. Images must be PNG, JPEG, or WebP and no larger than 3 MB. Uploaded photos are public catalog assets.

The ordered lineup determines what is available for the next auction. Reorder lots before the show, and keep prices and stock accurate.

## Offer buy-now

Set an optional buy-now price and enable **List in storefront**. A product being auctioned cannot also be purchased directly. Direct purchases require available stock and reserve one unit when the order is created. Live buy-now also requires configured Stripe Checkout.

## Photo to listing

Select Codex in Companion, then choose **Photo → listing** in Inventory. Provide a photo, opening price, quantity, confirmed condition, and notes. The server keeps your supplied price, quantity, condition, photo, and unlisted state on the draft.

Review every field in Companion. Use Edit draft before Apply when needed. A draft does not create inventory until you accept it. The model should describe visible features and your notes; it cannot establish authenticity or working condition.

## Import canonical card identity

Open Catalog or **Browse catalog**. Search, review a card's set and collector number, then enter your own opening price, quantity, and condition. Adding creates an unlisted lot. It does not start an auction or copy a market guide price. See [Catalog and Rip studio](/docs/streamer/catalog-and-rip).
