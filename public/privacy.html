Privacy policy

Updated October 25, 2026 · Version 2026-10-25

This notice describes the bundled software’s current data flows. Each installation operator must identify themselves below, publish any additional deployment practices and respond to privacy requests. Providing software does not automatically determine the parties’ legal privacy roles.

Who handles your information

The streamer/operator controls this installation and its connected accounts. The operator selects purposes, access, hosting and retention and must determine their obligations under applicable privacy law. A hosted deployment or additional service may involve other controllers/processors. The software does not identify a universal data controller for every deployment.

Information processed and purposes

Autoloot processes Twitch identifiers/usernames, registrations, chat received for the connected show, bids, voting records, listings and images, private orders, shipping/tracking details, payment references/status and contact details returned by checkout. These support account verification, show operation, fraud/moderation controls, payment reconciliation, fulfillment and support. Some chat may be recorded even when it does not place a bid. NFT asset identifiers, buyer-confirmed destination wallets, transfer intents and receipts are recorded for NFT orders. Operator settings and automation queues, policy acknowledgment timestamps and audit events are also persisted.

Shipping services

When the operator requests rates or labels, the recipient address, ship-from contact/address and parcel measurements are sent to Shippo and its carriers for shipping. Provider transaction references, label links, postage quotes/costs and tracking updates are stored privately with the order. Label links can reveal shipping addresses. Pirate Ship CSV export contains recipient addresses and is uploaded to Pirate Ship by the operator; this app does not connect to a Pirate Ship API. Shipping services process data under their own terms and privacy notices. Labels are separate from proof of package handoff.

Public information and private access

Storefronts and overlays expose listing information, product photos, bid usernames, auction outcomes, aggregate votes and cohost messages. Broadcast viewers may record or redistribute public material. Operator identity/contact and seller policies are intentionally public. Buyer orders, addresses, provider references and run definitions are excluded from public show snapshots. Authorized operators can access orders needed for fulfillment; buyer endpoints check ownership. Local machine or administrator compromise can expose stored data.

Storage, cookies and browser preferences

This version persists show/order data, registrations, activity, run audits, operator policies and uploaded catalog assets in the installation’s local data directory using SQLite, its journal/WAL files, uploaded files and database backups. SQLite data is not encrypted by this app; restrict filesystem access and use encrypted storage when appropriate. Operator-supplied Twitch application credentials and Shippo API tokens can be stored there as well as in environment configuration. Twitch authorization tokens are held in server memory. Browser sessions persist in SQLite with hashed session tokens, expiry and revocation. An HttpOnly session cookie supports sign-in; signing out clears the browser cookie but does not delete orders or other records. The selected widget preset and whether you started, skipped or completed the optional quick tour are saved in browser local storage when available. Network addresses and request metadata are used for connection/rate controls; hosting and external providers may keep their own logs.

The bundled app contains no advertising or third-party analytics tracker. This does not describe trackers or logging an operator independently adds, Twitch’s player/site, or external providers’ own practices. There is no cookie preference manager in this version.

AI, photographs, camera and screen sharing

Capture starts after a user selects a source and grants browser permission. A selected scan crop goes as a JPEG to the server and the Moondream vision provider selected by the streamer. Cloud mode sends frames to Prime Inference. Local mode sends frames only to the loopback Photon service on the same machine; it does not fall back to cloud. Uploaded listing photos may also be submitted for AI drafting and persist as public catalog assets. Autoloot and its bundled local vision adapter process scan frames in memory without retaining frame files. Cloud handling is governed by the provider’s policies. Stopping sharing ends browser capture tracks; it does not recall prior submissions.

Companion requests include the prompt, product/auction information, catalog context and limited order summaries. The built-in adapter excludes shipping addresses and service secrets from its intended AI context, but personal information or secrets typed into prompts or visible in submitted images can still be transmitted. Operator-supplied prompts and generated cohost messages may be displayed publicly. Data retention, training, international processing and other handling by the selected AI service depend on its terms and account configuration; this app does not promise zero retention or no training by that provider.

External services and recipients

Twitch handles OAuth and chat. Stripe handles hosted checkout when configured and returns order/payment information, buyer email and collected shipping information; raw payment-card numbers are not stored by this app. Metadata receives catalog queries, and external image hosts receive ordinary network requests, potentially including IP address. Codex uses the configured local account. When enabled, optional speech passes cohost text to the browser’s speech-synthesis service; device/voice-provider practices apply. For NFT delivery, the configured wallet service receives the exact asset, destination, delivery identifier and authorization limits; chain RPC services receive verification queries. Confirmed on-chain transfers and addresses are public and generally cannot be deleted from the blockchain by this app. Private wallet keys are not requested by the browser; signer credential/key handling belongs to the configured wallet service. Operators must disclose any additional service, export, contractor, hosting location or international transfer they use.

Retention and deletion

No automatic personal-data retention/deletion schedule is implemented. Reservation expiry, demo cleanup, signing out and disconnecting Twitch are not full data deletion. Records may remain in the data directory, uploaded files, CSV/JSON exports, run archives, temporary directories and backups. Operators must set an appropriate retention schedule, actually carry it out, secure copies and honor legal preservation duties. Provider-held records are governed by the provider’s practices and applicable rights. The operator’s notice below describes their intended policy; it is not an automatic deletion feature.

Your choices and rights

You can choose not to sign in, purchase, upload an image or enable capture; those choices may prevent the corresponding feature from working. Contact the operator for access, correction, deletion or other applicable rights. Some records may need retention for legal, payment or dispute obligations. The operator must identify any required lawful basis, consent mechanism, request/appeal route and regulator information for their deployment. Reading this notice is not blanket consent to all processing.

Eligibility, changes and security

The software is not designed for use by children to enter purchases or operate sales. Operators must enforce applicable eligibility, age and parental-consent requirements rather than relying on this statement alone. No system guarantees absolute security. Operators must provide any incident notices required by law and update this notice when their practices materially change.

Operator details

The streamer supplies the seller identity, support/privacy contact and transaction policies for this installation. Ask for those details before making a real purchase.
