Privacy policy

Updated October 24, 2026 · Local prototype

This notice describes the current Autoloot prototype. Before a public launch, the operator must add its legal name, contact address, retention schedule and any deployment-specific disclosures.

What Autoloot processes

Autoloot processes Twitch account IDs and usernames, registrations, relevant chat messages, bids, orders, shipping details, payment status and product information to run a stream’s sales and fulfillment. The streamer can see buyer and order information needed to manage the show. Public views include product listings, bid usernames, auction results and cohost messages; shipping addresses and private orders are restricted.

Storage and access

Show data, uploaded product images and app credentials are stored in the local installation’s data folder. Some operations create local backup files. Twitch authorization tokens and browser sessions are held in server memory. A session cookie keeps buyers signed in. Local administrator access controls the streamer dashboard; possession of local machine access can expose locally stored data.

Camera, screen and AI

Screen and camera capture starts only after you select a source. A scan sends a cropped JPEG to the local server and Codex for recognition. Temporary scan files are deleted after processing; the AI provider’s own data handling still applies. Uploaded product photos persist separately and can appear publicly in listings. Companion prompts include inventory, auction context, catalog results and limited order summaries; never enter secrets in chat. Companion messages may appear on the stream overlay.

External services

Twitch handles sign-in and stream chat. Stripe handles hosted checkout when configured; Autoloot stores payment references and fulfillment details, not raw payment-card numbers. Metadata receives catalog queries. Catalog images may load from external image hosts, exposing network information such as IP address to those hosts. Codex uses the configured local CLI account. These providers have their own privacy practices.

Your controls

Stop sharing to end capture, sign out to end a browser session, or disconnect Twitch in Settings. Removing demo content is not a full account or data deletion feature. For access, correction or deletion, contact the streamer or operator who supplied this installation. The current prototype has no automatic retention schedule; the operator must also review exports and backup copies when deleting data.

Questions

Contact the operator of the stream where you used Autoloot. A dedicated operator contact has not yet been configured for this prototype.
