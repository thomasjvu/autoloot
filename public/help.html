Help

Start a show

Open Settings to connect your Twitch app and authorize your channel. Add a lot manually or choose a card from Catalog. Set your opening price, quantity and condition, then start an auction in Studio.

Buy or bid

Open the streamer’s storefront, sign in and register before bidding. Enter !bid 25 in Twitch chat to bid $25. Check your private orders on the storefront for checkout and shipping updates.

Mochi

Use the bottom-right portrait for quick chat. Select Codex in Companion for AI features. Rip studio lets you share a camera or window and scan a reveal; review the match before announcing a pull.

Practice data

Settings lets you load sample lots or remove demo content. New installations start empty. Practice mode does not move real money.

Need support?

Contact the streamer for an order issue. For connection problems, open Settings and review the provider status. Catalog searches require an available, authorized Metadata API.
