Set up seller policies

In Settings → Seller identity & policies, publish the operator/seller name, public support/privacy email, shipping and returns policies and retention notice. These belong to the streamer running the installation. Review Terms and Privacy before real transactions.

Payment setup

During installation, add STRIPE_API_KEY and STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET to the server environment, then restart. Use a restricted Stripe key with Checkout, Payment Intents and Refunds access. Configure signed events at /api/stripe/webhook. Set your public HTTPS storefront URL and allowed shipping countries before real sales. Rehearse checkout and refunds in test mode first; live sales need live credentials.

Payment recovery

If a checkout or refund response is uncertain, stock or fulfillment stays held. Orders → Reconcile with Stripe lets the streamer verify an existing Checkout session or refund ID against the provider. Do not create a second payment or transfer while an outcome is unknown. Definitively failed refunds leave the original payment collected and permit a new refund attempt.

Stream setup

Connect your channel through Twitch setup. In Settings, choose an OBS layout and copy its link into an OBS Browser Source using the displayed width and height. Use one voice-enabled source to avoid duplicate speech.

Buy or bid

Review the identified seller’s policies, sign in with Twitch and use !bid 25.00 in the connected chat. The server controls bid acceptance; video may be delayed. Your private orders show checkout, a reservation deadline and shipping updates. A payment return page alone does not prove payment.

AI and agent-led runs

Munny is a software cohost. AI replies and card matches can be wrong; confirm important information with the seller. Choose I lead, Check with me, or Keep it moving in Settings. Check with me asks for each lot’s approval; your run can override that choice. Operators can arm a bounded queue in Studio; sale rules are enforced by the engine. Physical opening and shipping still require the operator. Open-ended AI proposals need review and Apply.

Privacy and capture

Enable camera/screen capture only when needed and avoid other people’s personal information. Stop sharing or leaving Rip Studio ends capture. Signing out or removing demo content does not delete all stored data; contact the operator for privacy requests.

Practice and support

Practice payments are simulated. For order, shipping, refund or privacy issues, contact the seller/operator below. For connection problems, review Settings. Provider disputes and statutory remedies remain available where applicable.

Operator details

The streamer supplies the seller identity, support/privacy contact and transaction policies for this installation. Ask for those details before making a real purchase.
