Using Autoloot

Autoloot helps streamers manage listings, auctions, buyer orders and an optional AI cohost. Use it only if you are authorized to operate the connected accounts and legally able to enter the transactions you initiate. Keep your app keys and local administrator access private.

Listings and purchases

The seller is responsible for item ownership, accurate descriptions, condition, authenticity claims, pricing, taxes, shipping, returns and customer support. Review each listing and its seller’s policies before bidding. Autoloot does not independently authenticate cards, assign grades or guarantee market values.

Auction rules

Accepted bids must meet the opening price and increment. The server determines bid receipt order and the winner. Standard auctions reset the remaining clock to ten seconds when a bid is accepted with fewer than ten seconds left. Sudden-death auctions have a fixed deadline. Network latency can prevent a bid from arriving in time. A winning bid creates an order; payment status is tracked separately. The streamer can pause or cancel auctions.

Practice mode

Sample inventory and demo payments are simulations. Reference images do not represent seller-owned stock. Coming-soon features are plans and are not available transaction formats.

AI and pricing data

Card recognition, generated listing drafts and cohost replies can be inaccurate. Review proposed actions and card matches. Market quotes are dated guides, may be stale, and are not guaranteed sale prices or investment advice.

Acceptable use

Do not submit unlawful listings, fraudulent bids, counterfeit ownership claims, abusive content or data you lack permission to share. Do not attempt to access another buyer’s private information or interfere with the auction system.

Availability and disputes

This prototype may be interrupted or changed. Contact the seller for item, shipping, refund and payment questions. Provider services remain subject to their own terms. Nothing in these prototype terms removes rights that applicable law does not allow to be waived.
